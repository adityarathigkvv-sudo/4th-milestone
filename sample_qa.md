# Sample Q&A

### 1. What is the exit load of SBI Large Cap Fund?
SBI Large Cap Fund has an exit load of 0.25% within 30 days, 0.10% after 30 days and within 90 days, and nil after 90 days from allotment.
Source: https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/all-sbimf-schemes-factsheet-february-2026.pdf?sfvrsn=94669a9a_4
Last updated from sources: 28 February 2026

### 2. What is the minimum SIP for SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund?
SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund has a minimum SIP amount of ₹500, in multiples of ₹500, for the stated SIP frequencies.
Source: https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/sbi-elss-tax-saver-fund-factsheet-february-2026.pdf?sfvrsn=1c3c7545_2
Last updated from sources: 28 February 2026

### 3. What is the benchmark of SBI Flexicap Fund?
The first-tier benchmark for SBI Flexicap Fund is BSE 500 (TRI).
Source: https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/sbi-flexicap-fund-factsheet-february-2026.pdf?sfvrsn=583b8e9e_2
Last updated from sources: 28 February 2026

### 4. What is the lock-in period of SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund?
SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund has a statutory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of allotment.
Source: https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/sbi-elss-tax-saver-fund-factsheet-february-2026.pdf?sfvrsn=1c3c7545_2
Last updated from sources: 28 February 2026

### 5. What is the expense ratio of SBI Large Cap Fund?
The February 2026 SBI MF TER document lists 1.47% for the Regular Plan and 0.79% for the Direct Plan.
Source: https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/all-sbimf-schemes-factsheet-february-2026.pdf?sfvrsn=94669a9a_4
Last updated from sources: 28 February 2026

### 6. Can you tell me which fund I should buy?
I’m a facts-only assistant, so I can’t recommend, rank, or tell you whether to buy, sell, switch, or choose a fund. I can provide factual details such as expense ratio, exit load, minimum SIP, lock-in, benchmark and riskometer.
Source: https://www.sbimf.com/
Last updated from sources: Current public page

### 7. How do I get my capital gains statement?
SBI Mutual Fund says investors can access Account Statement, Capital Gains Statement and Smart Statement through its investor services.
Source: https://www.sbimf.com/ways-to-invest
Last updated from sources: Current public page

### 8. What is the riskometer?
SEBI describes the Riskometer as a standardized tool showing a mutual-fund scheme's risk level from Low to Very High.
Source: https://investor.sebi.gov.in/riskometer.html
Last updated from sources: Current public page
