# 3-minute demo script

0:00–0:20 — Show the landing screen.
Say: “This is SBI MF FactsBot, a Groww-focused facts-only RAG assistant. It covers three SBI Mutual Fund schemes and uses only approved official SBI MF and SEBI sources.”

0:20–1:00 — Ask: “What is the exit load of SBI Large Cap Fund?”
Show the concise answer and the single official source link.

1:00–1:30 — Ask: “What is the minimum SIP for SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund?”
Show the answer and dated source.

1:30–2:00 — Ask: “What is the expense ratio of SBI Flexicap Fund?”
Show Regular and Direct TER from the official February 2026 TER document.

2:00–2:25 — Ask: “Which fund should I buy?”
Show the facts-only refusal.

2:25–2:45 — Ask a PII-containing question, e.g. one containing a fake PAN.
Show that the app blocks personal/financial identifiers.

2:45–3:00 — Open README/source list.
Say: “The submission includes 20 official public URLs, sample Q&A, disclaimer, corpus and setup instructions.”
